## Description (4000 chars)

```
Unspend helps you take your time back from the apps that quietly eat your day.

Pick the apps that distract you. Set a rule. Unspend uses iOS Screen Time at the system level so the block is real — not a willpower test you'll fail at 11pm.

WAYS TO BLOCK

• Manual — tap to block, tap to unblock (with a cooldown + PIN so you can't cave instantly).
• Schedule — block during work hours, study time, after bedtime.
• Daily budget — give yourself 20 minutes of social. After that, blocked until tomorrow.
• Task mode — block until your to-dos are done. Check them off to earn access.

STACK THEM

Combine any of the above. "Block Instagram between 9 and 5, AND only after I've done my three tasks, AND with a 30-minute daily ceiling." Unspend stacks the rules — strictest one wins.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud.
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
• Your data stays on your device. Period.
• Your PIN is hashed and stored in the iOS Keychain — not even Unspend can read it.

REQUIRES iOS 16+

Unspend uses Apple's Family Controls framework to enforce blocks. You'll be prompted to authorize it once on first use. It's a self-blocking tool, not a parental-controls app.

AVAILABLE IN

English, German, Spanish, French, Croatian.

—

Built solo. Designed for people who already know they have a phone problem and just want a tool that actually works.
```
