Privacy Policy — Unspend

Last updated: 12 May 2026

Unspend is built to help you take back your time. We do that by giving you tools that run entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your personal data.

What we store, and where

  • Blocking profiles, schedules, tasks, and stats are saved using on-device SharedPreferences.
  • Your PIN is hashed (SHA-256 with a per-install random salt) and stored in the iOS Keychain. The PIN itself never leaves your device, and the hash is unreadable without your PIN.
  • The list of apps you choose to block is held by Apple's FamilyControls framework. iOS manages this directly; we cannot read, export, or share the list, and neither can anyone else.

What we don't do

  • No analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or telemetry.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No network requests for user data — the app runs offline.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no SDKs that could share data with third parties.
  • No use of IDFA / App Tracking Transparency — we don't track you across apps or websites.

Family Controls / Screen Time API

Unspend requests Family Controls authorization so iOS can enforce your self-imposed blocks. This authorization is requested on first use and stays local to your device. We use this API only to:

  • Read which apps and categories you've chosen to block (you select them via Apple's system picker — the selection never leaves Apple's framework).
  • Apply and remove iOS shields when you activate or deactivate a profile.
  • Schedule time-based blocks and usage limits through Apple's DeviceActivity framework.

Your control

You can delete every byte of data Unspend has stored on your device at any time: open the app, go to Settings → Delete All Data. This removes all profiles, your PIN, the daily activity log, and any pending shields. Uninstalling the app removes everything iOS keeps for the app as well.

Children

Unspend is a self-blocking productivity tool. It is not directed at children, and it does not function as a parental-controls app. We don't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data we'll update this page and, where required, prompt you inside the app on next launch.
